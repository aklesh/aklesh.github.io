No one person can tell you what fieldwork is like. Antarctica is different from a research vessel; a research vessel is different from a volcano; and a six-week field camp is different from a two-day deployment.

I’ve collected a few field diaries and expedition journals from scientists, engineers, and explorers whose experiences I think are worth reading. Some are highly technical. Others are mostly about the people and places. All of them offer something that a resume or project report can’t: a glimpse of what it is actually like to be in the field.

Want another perspective?

  • MLParker Media - Ocean Going Field Notes
  • NASA Notes from the Field - 88S Expedition
  • ESA Campaign Earth — Diary of a Polar Scientist

There are many additional field notes, diaries, logs, and mission blogs from other expeditions, campaigns, and flights. Search around - you never know what kind of tale you’ll find!

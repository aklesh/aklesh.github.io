This Website

This website site is a personal endeavor and is not supported by any organization. The views, information, and opinions expressed on this site are solely my own and may not represent those of my employer. Citations and references are provided for source material and/or ownership attribution.

These pages were created, in part, as an experiment with AI tools, including Google Antigravity, Claude, and ChatGPT. The project serves as an investigation of how AI agents can be utilized to amplify productivity and execute a vision without the author needing detailed knowledge of Javascript, CSS, and HTML.

What have I learned about AI? It has been extremely useful for backend work or page design, but will often hallucinate when trying to "help" write content (e.g., suggesting that ARIA stands for Aerial Recovery of Interplanetary Assets apparently!). It also defaults to common phrasing or words and always provides extremely positive feedback. That being said, AI tools have been very useful as a scratchpad or to bounce ideas around. Will I keep using it? Yes - with careful supervision!

I commonly use Amazon links as an aid to the viewer (especially on the gear pages), but I am not an affiliate and do not gain anything from them.

Content & Authorship

All content, design direction, and aesthetics were provided by the author, though the backend infrastructure and code were assisted by AI. Human-authored materials include:

  • All personal photos and expedition media.
  • Curated lists of field opportunities and student resources.
  • Selection and categorization of specific research publications and references.
  • Detailed course descriptions and pedagogical philosophy.

Some pages were initially drafted with AI summaries.

Any external materials or guides included on this site are appropriately referenced to their original authors and institutions to ensure students have direct access to primary sources.

How it was Built

The technical foundation of this site—including the Python-based static site generator, the modular template system, and the automated citation processing—was developed with the support of AI. This allowed for a rapid translation of high-level goals into a fully functional web platform.

Open-source libraries were used to build the site, including:

  • Python - site generation
  • Markdown - content formatting
  • Bootstrap - responsive design
  • StackEdit - markdown editor
  • CARTO - interactive maps

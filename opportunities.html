Field Opportunities

Credit: Andrew Klesh

This page provides a few links for students to reference for field opportunities. Whether through a field program, through lab classes, through research projects, or on their own time, I strongly encourage students to obtain hands-on experience in the field with practical problems and investigations.

Please note: Students should always contact the program directly for any details as the information below has been collected on a best effort basis.

📅 Rows with a known application deadline include a calendar icon. Clicking it downloads a reminder event set two weeks before that deadline, so you have time to actually finish and submit your application rather than being reminded on the day it's due.

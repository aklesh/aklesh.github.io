Andy Klesh

Dr. Andrew Klesh is a project systems engineer at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory and a researcher at the California Institute of Technology. His work focuses on exploration, the use of small vehicles to provide unique vantages, and pushing the boundaries of what is possible in extreme environments.

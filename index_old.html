Meet Andy

Exploring extreme environments means something always breaks. My teams and I chase hard science questions — what's inside a volcano, what's alive under the ice — heading to the field with the custom instruments and robots it takes to go find out, whether that's in the deep ocean or on spacecraft headed elsewhere. Something breaks. We adapt. We make the science happen.

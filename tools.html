Useful Tools

These tools will assist with expedition planning and execution.

Shipping Label Generator →

A simple tool to generate shipping labels for your expeditions.

Reconn - Backcountry Itinerary Generator →

Guides you through the process of creating a backcountry itinerary note that can be left with friends or family and on your car's dashboard prior to a backcountry trip. Assists Search and Rescue if you are delayed on your return.

Gaia GPS →

Provides maps and GPS tracks. Many map layers are available including topographic maps, satellite imagery, land use maps, and weather. Can tag photos with GPS coordinates and import/export GPX files.

HabHub - High Altitude Balloon Planning and Tracking →

A suite of tools for planning and tracking your high altitude balloon flights.

DAS Hub - Distributed Acoustic Sensing →

Tools and data from DAS deployments, including a live feed of the Southern California Seismic Network's Ridgecrest DAS array.

Squoosh - Image Optimization →

A web app that allows for different image formats and experimenting with compression.
